# Skori — Food & Cosmetic Scanner

Skori is a bilingual (Arabic / English) app for Saudi Arabia that scores food and cosmetic products.
Scan a barcode and get a 0–100 score, plus the exact ingredients that moved it and the evidence behind each flag.

- 📱 Live on iOS and Android
- 🌐 Arabic and English, including Arabic labels
- 🛒 A catalogue of 50,000+ products

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/sa/app/skori-food-scanner/id6760096881) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.skori.app) · [skoriapp.com](https://skoriapp.com)

## How it works

- Flutter app with a Firebase backend.
- Claude reads ingredient and nutrition labels from user photos to add missing products.
- Scores are rule-based, and every flag links to its evidence.
